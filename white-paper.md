The conditions AI needs are the same ones agile asked for a decade ago. Most organisations ran the ceremonies and skipped the redesign. They are being asked the same question again now with higher stakes and less time to answer it.

This paper sets out the full argument and the evidence behind it: why BCG's research puts 70% of the AI challenge on people and process and 10% on the algorithms, what that means in practice across six specific organisational domains, and why the gap between where a business thinks it is and where it actually is tends to be widest in the places nobody is looking.
